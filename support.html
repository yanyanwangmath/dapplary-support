Dapplary Support

Have a question, found something that isn’t working, or want to share an idea? You can reach us here.

Email Dapplary support

dapplary.support@pm.me

What to include

Tell us what you were trying to do, what happened, and what you expected. If you can, include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and Dapplary version. A screenshot can help; hide any private text or photos first.

You do not need to send your journal, a password, or payment details. Support is provided by the developer, Yanyan Wang.

Where are my moments stored?

Your moments and optional photos are saved on your device. Dapplary has no account or cloud-sync service, so they do not automatically appear on another device. Apple-managed device backups depend on your system settings; they are separate from Dapplary.

If your entries seem to be missing, avoid deleting the app or clearing its data. Close and reopen the app first. If the issue continues, email us with what happened before the entries disappeared. We can help investigate, but we do not have a server copy to restore.

Can I use Dapplary offline?

Yes. The idea library, journaling, and saved moments are available without an internet connection. Opening these support pages or sending email requires a connection.

How do I add a photo?

When creating a moment, choose Choose from photos or Take a photo. A photo is optional. If camera access was denied, review Dapplary’s permissions in iOS Settings, then try again.

How do I delete my data?

Open the app’s privacy view, choose Delete all local data, and confirm with Delete permanently. This removes all local moments, photos, custom ideas, saved ideas, plans, and the unfinished draft. Dapplary cannot undo this action. Manage any existing Apple device backups separately in your system settings.

Privacy questions

Read the Privacy Policy for details about local storage, backups, support email, and this website. You can also email us with a privacy question or a request about support correspondence.
