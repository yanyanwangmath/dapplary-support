Dapplary Support

Have a question, found something that isn’t working, or want to share an idea? You can reach us here.

Email Dapplary support

dapplary.support@pm.me

What to include

Tell us what you were trying to do, what happened, and what you expected. If you can, include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and Dapplary version. A screenshot can help; hide any private text or photos first.

You do not need to send your journal, a password, or payment details. Support is provided by the developer, Yanyan Wang.

Where are my moments stored?

Your moments and optional photos are saved on your device. Dapplary has no account or cloud-sync service, so they do not automatically appear on another device. Apple-managed device backups depend on your system settings; they are separate from Dapplary.

If your entries seem to be missing, avoid deleting the app or clearing its data. Close and reopen the app first. If the issue continues, email us with what happened before the entries disappeared. We can help investigate, but we do not have a server copy to restore.

Can I use Dapplary offline?

Yes. The idea library, journaling, and saved moments are available without an internet connection. Opening these support pages or sending email requires a connection.

How do I find something small to try?

Choose Just five minutes or A quiet moment on Today or Discovery for three ideas to try at home. Use Refresh ideas to see another selection or Browse all ideas to return to the full library. Today’s inspiration follows your device’s local time; Something easier offers a free, solo idea taking five minutes or less.

How do I plan an idea?

Browse 30 included ideas in Discovery, or create a private idea of your own. Choose Try this, pick Today, This week, or Someday, and save the plan. Your plans appear on Today, with today’s plans first, then this week’s, followed by later and earlier intentions. Tap the timing to change it, choose Cancel plan to remove it, or Keep a moment to record what happened. The plan is cleared only after your moment is saved. These are gentle intentions, without deadlines or reminder notifications.

Do I have to choose a feeling?

No. A sentence or a photo is enough. Feeling, place, and date are optional details; feelings start as Not added. You can choose Okay or Mixed as well as a positive feeling. If another unfinished moment is waiting, Dapplary lets you continue it before starting from a different idea.

Why does an older moment appear on Today?

Once you have kept at least three moments, Today can bring back one of your entries from at least a week ago. Tap it to open the original memory. Your collection and monthly recaps remain in Memories.

How do I add a photo?

When creating a moment, choose Choose from photos or Take a photo. A photo is optional. If camera access was denied, review Dapplary’s permissions in iOS Settings, then try again.

Can I continue an unfinished moment?

Yes. Text, optional details, and the selected photo are kept as a draft on this device when you close the moment form. Choose Continue your moment on Today to pick up the draft where you left off. Use Remove photo to change the attachment or Discard draft to remove the unfinished entry. A draft is cleared when the moment is successfully saved.

How do I make a memory book?

Open Memories → Make a memory book and select up to 30 memories. Nothing is selected automatically. Your book arranges the chosen moments from oldest to newest. Choose For myself or For someone, add a title and an optional recipient name, dedication, and sign-off, then choose Linen or Sage. Memory dates are off unless you turn on Include memory dates.

Choose Preview book to review each page. Use Edit book to make changes. On iOS, Save or share PDF opens the system share sheet: choose Save to Files, Print, or a sharing destination. In a browser, Print / Save PDF opens the print dialog. Nothing is sent automatically. Closing the editor clears the unfinished book, while your saved memories stay unchanged.

How should I print my book?

The PDF contains individual A5 portrait pages, 148 × 210 mm. Choose A5 paper and 100% scale or Actual size when printing. It uses RGB colour, with no bleed or printer’s marks, and is not arranged for folding into a booklet. If a print shop needs CMYK colour, bleed, or booklet layout, ask them to prepare the file first. Photo sharpness depends on the resolution of the images saved with your memories.

A book PDF is a separate, readable copy of the memories you selected. Review it before sharing and keep private copies somewhere you trust. It cannot be imported to restore your collection; use Backup & restore for a restorable backup.

How do I make a backup?

Open Settings → Backup & restore → Export backup, then choose Save to Files in the iOS share sheet. The readable JSON file includes saved moments and their photos, custom ideas, bookmarks, and plans with their selected timing. Unfinished drafts are not included. Keep this private file somewhere you trust.

To restore, choose Choose backup file, review the counts, and select Add to my collection. Existing records stay unchanged and missing items are added, so importing the same backup again does not duplicate entries. Backups from earlier Dapplary versions still work; plans without a saved time appear under Someday. If a plan already exists, restoring preserves its current timing. Restoring an older backup can bring back a moment you deleted after exporting it. If a file is damaged or unsupported, it is rejected before changing your collection.

How do I edit or delete one moment?

Open the moment from Today or Memories. Choose Edit this moment to correct its words, feeling, place, or date while keeping its photo. Choose Delete this moment and confirm to remove that record and photo from the app. Other moments stay unchanged.

How do I delete my data?

Open the app’s privacy view, choose Delete all local data, and confirm with Delete permanently. This removes all local moments, photos, custom ideas, saved ideas, plans, and the unfinished draft. Dapplary cannot undo this action directly. Copies in previously exported files remain separate; manage or delete those files yourself. Manage any existing Apple device backups separately in your system settings.
