Privacy Policy

Effective 12 September 2026 · Dapplary for iOS, version 1.0

Yanyan Wang (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides Dapplary, a personal journaling and reflection app. This policy explains how information is handled in the app, when you contact support, and on this website.

The essentials. No account is required. Your journal entries and optional photos stay in the app’s local storage. Dapplary does not send this content to us and has no advertising, analytics, cloud sync, or public feed.

What you save in the app

You may add journal text, dates, places, moods, custom ideas, saved ideas, plans, drafts, and optional photos. The app processes and stores these on your device. A place is something you enter; Dapplary does not request your device’s location. We do not operate a server that receives your journal or use it to train AI models.

Camera and photos

Adding a photo is optional. The camera or photo picker opens after you choose the corresponding action. Dapplary uses the image you capture or select, resizes it locally, and stores it with your entry. The app does not intentionally save a captured photo to your photo library or upload it to us. You can review camera and photo permissions in iOS Settings.

Storage, backups, and deletion

Your content remains in local app storage until you delete it or that storage is removed. In Dapplary’s privacy view, choose Delete all local data and confirm to remove the moments, photos, custom ideas, saved ideas, plans, and unfinished draft held by that installation. Deleting the app can also remove its local data. Offloading an app is different and may preserve its documents and data.

Dapplary has no account-based recovery or cloud-sync service. Depending on your device and iCloud Backup settings, Apple may include app data in a device backup and restore it later. We cannot access, manage, or delete those backups; manage them through Apple’s settings. Deleting local data does not itself delete an existing device backup. We keep no server copy and cannot recover your entries remotely.

When you email support

If you email dapplary.support@pm.me, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to answer your request and investigate problems. Please avoid sending private journal content, passwords, or other information that is not needed to explain the issue.

Support email is handled through Proton Mail, whose processing is described in Proton’s privacy policy. We retain support correspondence only as needed to resolve and follow up on the request, maintain necessary support records, or meet applicable legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your correspondence at the same address. We do not use support messages for marketing or sell them.

Visiting this website

These support and privacy pages use no analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms, or third-party fonts. They are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as described in its Pages documentation and privacy statement. Opening an external link is separate from storing your journal in the app.

Platform services and security

Apple may process information for App Store distribution, device backups, diagnostics, or crash reporting under its own settings and privacy policy. These services do not give us access to your local journal or selected photos.

Local storage limits transmission, but no storage method is completely secure. Use a device passcode and keep your operating system updated.

Your choices

You control what you enter, whether you attach photos, device permissions, and deletion of local data. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold in support correspondence, restrict or object to its use, or raise a concern with a privacy authority. Contact us to make a request. We cannot access or identify the journal stored only on your device.

Children

Dapplary does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the app. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through support email, contact us so we can address it.

Updates and contact

If these practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date. Any future accounts, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, or sharing features would require an updated explanation before release.
